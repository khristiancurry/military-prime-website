Ask us directly
How quickly will my property be rented?+

It depends on current market conditions, comparable homes, how your property shows, and how it's priced against the competition. In a normal rental market, a well-presented, properly priced home in the Fredericksburg area will attract interested prospects.

What determines the rent for my property?+

Rental prices are determined by the market and the owner. We make a recommendation based on current conditions and, when available, rental pricing in nearby subdivisions over the past year. You determine the final rent.

Who pays for marketing my property?+

Military Prime covers marketing costs, including signage, labor and online advertising. Those expenses are included in the leasing fee.

How should I prepare my property to be rented?+

Make the property move-in ready, clean and in good condition. A home that shows well is more attractive to prospective tenants and can rent more quickly.

How are tenants selected?+

We review each application, verify rental history and employment, and use national reports for credit, criminal history and evictions. We also speak briefly with prospective tenants to review their application and answer questions.

Who completes repairs if work is needed?+

We work with contractors who are licensed and familiar with the professional standards we require at our properties.

Who pays for maintenance and repairs?+

The owner pays for maintenance and repairs. If a repair is the tenant's responsibility under the lease agreement, the tenant is responsible for that cost.
