Patie Grigsby
A seasoned professional with over 25 years of experience in the building industry, specializing in sales, customer service, and client support. Her extensive knowledge and hands-on expertise make her a trusted resource for managing a wide range of service needs with efficiency, care, and professionalism.

Patie is passionate about delivering exceptional service and building lasting relationships with both clients and team members. She approaches every challenge with clear communication, practical problem-solving, and a deep understanding of the building process from the ground up.

Originally from Pennsylvania, Patie chose to stay in the area after graduating from the University of Mary Washington. She now lives in Spotsylvania County with her three daughters and several four-legged companions. A dedicated animal lover, she also volunteers with a local animal rescue. Whether in her professional life or personal commitments, Patie leads with heart, accountability, and an unwavering commitment to excellence.
