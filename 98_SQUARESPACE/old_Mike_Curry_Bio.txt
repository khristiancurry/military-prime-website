Mike Curry
First of all, thanks for taking the time to read about me. My name is Mike Curry and I’ll start off by telling you a little about myself. After growing up in Stafford/Fredericksburg, my family and I now call Spotsylvania home. I am proud to say I have been married since 1994 and am the proud father of 3 boys, 2 of which graduated from Clemson. For eleven years we had only the three boys, and then we got the most awesome gift anyone could want with that much testosterone in the family…a little girl! I can also say I am privileged enough to have had the opportunity to coach all 3 boys in high school football. After coaching high school football for 8 years, I finally decided to hang up my whistle and watch from the stands. Our family enjoys boating, golf, and of course, watching football!

Having been a licensed REALTOR® since 2002, I’ve won many awards for being a high-producing agent. Of them all, my most cherished are my pinnacle of quality service awards. I say that not to impress you, but to let you know that there are two things that I feel have always kept my clients sending me referrals and giving me repeat business, and that’s:

1. I am completely and totally 100% honest with you and if I talk myself out of a commission, then so be it. I am a firm believer in whatever is meant to be and if I feel it would be better for you to rent your home than sell it, I’ll tell you.

2. I am an extremely low-pressure agent. I will be aggressive for you on your behalf, but I will not be aggressive with you. The days of pressuring consumers into a transaction are over. I firmly uphold that because of these 2 reasons, my clients trust me with their homes, and trust me with their friends, families, and co-workers. I would love to help you out any way I can, even if it’s simply answering a question. Any way that I can help you, I will.

As broker/owner of Military Prime, my primary focus is ensuring that our clients, owners, and tenants have the best real estate experience possible. You are the most important part of our company and we strive to always show you that. We know and believe that we are here for you, you are not here for us. Our company would not be where it is today if it weren’t for you!

You served us, now let us serve you!
